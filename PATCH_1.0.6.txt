Nyama+ v1.0.7

- Android TV sidebar no longer gets stuck on TV.
- UP/DOWN explicitly selects TV / Movies / Series / Settings.
- CENTER/ENTER opens the selected sidebar destination.

- Sidebar fully expanded at the leftmost level; categories/channels fold progressively as focus moves right.
- Moving left unfolds the previous menu level.
- Mobile layout/navigation left unchanged.
