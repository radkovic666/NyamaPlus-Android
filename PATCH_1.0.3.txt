Nyama+ v1.0.5

Changes:
- Live TV browser now uses a dedicated left sidebar similar to the catalog screens.
- TV / Movies / Series / Settings are no longer mixed into the TV category list.
- BACK while watching TV opens the opaque browser menu with live TV still playing behind it.
- MENU/SETTINGS opens the same menu focused on the sidebar.
- Existing TV behaviour, playback logic and movie player behaviour remain unchanged.
