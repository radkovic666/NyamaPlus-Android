Nyama+ v1.0.7

Changes:
- Movies/Series use more of the TV screen with 30-item pages, at least four columns on TV and smaller cards.
- Posters use FIT_CENTER so poster headers/footers are not cropped.
- Catalog sidebar starts collapsed to icons and expands/collapses smoothly with left/right TV navigation.
- Removed Pukanki.Fun branding from Movies/Series headers.
- Movies and Series remember their own search text, page, sorting, favorites-only filter, genres and selected position after switching to TV or another section.
- Filter dialog redesigned with separate Favorites, Sort and Genres sections.
- Watch/Favorite buttons have stronger focus highlights.
- Movie player adds remote controls for play/pause, -10/+10 seconds, subtitles, quality/settings bridge, fullscreen and media keys.
