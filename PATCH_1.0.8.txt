Nyama+ v1.0.8 / versionCode 9

Android TV patch
- Movies and Series cards now reserve a real information area below each poster and show the available catalog metadata instead of clipping it.
- WATCH / FAVORITE buttons on the title page are slightly smaller on TV.
- Player 2 remote input is intercepted before WebView at dispatchKeyEvent level.
- When Player 2 controls are visible, LEFT/UP and RIGHT/DOWN move through controls and OK activates the focused control.
- When controls are hidden, OK/UP/DOWN opens the native Player 2 control strip; LEFT/RIGHT still seeks -10/+10 seconds.
- The player gives the largest video/iframe surface focus after loading and after closing the native controls.
- Sidebar TV/Movies/Series/Settings icons use TV vector drawables sized to fit the collapsed rail, fixing clipped icons.
- Movies and Series search has an X inside the field to clear the query.
- These UI/navigation changes are Android TV-only; phone/mobile catalog/detail/player behavior is retained.
