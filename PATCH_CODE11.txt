Nyama+ v1.0.9 - Code 11

Scoped changes only:
- Android TV: refreshed TV / Movies / Series / Settings navigation icons.
- Phone portrait only: redesigned the live-TV browser geometry so the app menu, categories,
  channel list and EPG information no longer overlap on narrow screens. No phone playback,
  backend or catalog behavior was changed.
- Android TV Movies/Series: exact visible title count and paging after posterless titles are
  removed. Posterless entries remain hidden.
- Android TV Movies/Series cards: poster + title + year only.
- Android TV title detail: complete catalog metadata is moved into the title window, including
  year, runtime, rating, readable vote count, genres and series season/episode information.
- Android TV Movie/Series player: removed the Nyama+ internal player-control bar and removed all
  TAB-key emulation. DPAD/OK are delivered to the embedded player directly. TV playback helper
  requests autoplay and prefers Bulgarian native/select/custom subtitle controls whenever the embedded player
  exposes them to Android System WebView.

No playlist, EPG, live-TV playback, authentication, update, admin/backend URL, or other unrelated
logic was intentionally changed.
